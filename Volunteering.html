Volunteering and Leadership

Golden Key International Honor Society - Leadership

In 2023, I was selected as a Golden Hand for the Golden Key International
Honour Society (2022/2023 term),supporting the executive committee across
events and initiatives.This experience deepened my commitment to service
and leadership, and later that year I was appointed Campus and Community 
Outreach Director for the 2023/2024 executive term — a natural progression
of my previous role, now with direct responsibility for stakeholder
engagement. In this capacity, I worked hands-on within the community,
organizing outreach events and driving initiatives aimed
at empowering those around me.






North-West University - Volunteering 

In my final year (2025), I wanted to challenge myself with something
outside my comfort zone — something that also tied directly into my
career path.That's when I became a Supplemental Instructor (SI) for
Linear Algebra. As an SI, I facilitated peer-led study sessions,
breaking down complex concepts for students struggling with the
material, and helped build a collaborative learning environment
outside the traditional lecture setting.It pushed me to strengthen
not just my technical knowledge, but also my ability to teach,
communicate, and support others.
