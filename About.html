ABOUT ME 

    
    I am OBOIKANYO PHIRI — a young lady who grew up in Protea Glen, Soweto.
    I come from a family of 5, including me, 
    with 2 younger brothers coming after me and both supporting parents.

    I went to Pentarose Primary School in Lenasia,
    and thereafter attended Topaz Secondary School, where I completed my matric in 2020.

    In 2021, I took an unplanned gap year and started with a bit of programming — with zero
    coding skills,just an eagerness to learn something new while applying for university.
    I did online courses and gained a bit of understanding, which led me to apply for
    Computer Science and Mathematics(already having a pure mathematics background from high school) atNorth-West university
    In 2022 that is when the journey became even more exciting, as I got closer to my dreams.

    Beyond the degree, university gave me something just as valuable — the people. I made 
    friends who became family, and through them (and the challenges we faced together), 
    I discovered a lot about myself: what I value, how I handle pressure, and just how far a
    little determination can take you. I also made sure to get the full campus experience —
    attending events, getting involved beyond the classroom, and soaking up everything that
    student life had to offer.In 2025, all of that hard work, growth, 
    and unforgettable experience came full circle when I graduated.
